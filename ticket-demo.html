Questions people actually ask

Still curious? Here's the rundown.

Can I get a refund if I can't make it? +

Seats are refundable up to 24 hours before doors open. After that, your hold can still be transferred to a friend — just show them the code.

Is there an age restriction? +

Most shows are 18+. Sunday matinee slots and select Open Mic nights are all-ages — check the tag on the ticket card.

What time should I actually show up? +

Doors open 30 minutes before showtime. Seating is first-come within your ticket tier, so earlier gets you closer to the stage.

What happens if my hold expires? +

The seat drops back into the pool for other fans. You can just claim a fresh ticket if one's still available.
